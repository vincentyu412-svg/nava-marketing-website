Gym growth reading order Read it in the order members arrive.

A new member passes four checkpoints: they find you, they enquire, they book and turn up, and they sign and stay. Most gyms lose people at one checkpoint more than the rest. Start with the stage where yours leak, then work outwards; the way we run it for our gyms follows the same order.

    Get found

    People search “gym near me” long before they fill in a form. Your Google profile is the shop window they check first.

    Book and show

    Reach every enquiry within minutes, say the right thing on the phone, and give them an intro worth turning up for.

    Sign and stay
