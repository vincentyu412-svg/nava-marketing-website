Gym workflow automation Eight workflows, one pipeline.

None of this is clever, and that's the point. It's the work almost nobody does consistently by hand, so we make sure no one has to. Every workflow below is built inside your CRM, written for your gym and tested on a real phone before it touches a real lead. You approve the wording before anything goes live.

01 · Instant first touch

  1. 01

    Instant first touch

    Every lead source, whether a Facebook form, your website, a Google call or a walk-in, is wired to one trigger that fires the moment a lead arrives, from a local number in your gym's name. What that first message says and how fast it lands is our gym speed-to-lead follow-up; the workflow makes sure it fires every time.

  2. 02

    The multi-week sequence

    The timing logic behind the follow-up: waits, conditions and stop rules, so the sequence can run for weeks without anyone scheduling it and halts the instant someone replies or books. Most gyms give up after a couple of attempts. The system doesn't get bored.

  3. 03

    Booking and reminders

    A calendar with your real availability that leads can book themselves into, wired to the pipeline so every booking moves the lead to booked and queues its confirmation and reminders. What those reminders say, and the intro that follows, is our gym sign-ups service. In group fitness studios, where every booking is a spot in a class, this is what keeps the class full.

  4. 04

    No-show recovery

    Someone books, then life gets in the way. When an intro is missed, a friendly reschedule offer with new times goes out on its own, so the lead goes back on the calendar instead of into the lost column. No-shows are often a bigger leak than bad leads.

  5. 05

    Lead tracking in one pipeline

    Every lead is stamped with its source and moves through stages that match how your gym sells. Tasks land on the right person's phone when a human is needed, so you can tell an ad lead from a walk-in and see who is waiting on whom.

  6. 06

    Reactivation

    Old enquiries, expired trials and members who left on good terms sit in a spreadsheet doing nothing. We import and clean the list, then run a carefully written reactivation campaign. You already paid for those leads once.

  7. 07

    Missed-call text-back

    When a call to your gym goes unanswered, a text goes out from your number within moments, so the caller hears back instead of dialling the next gym on the list. It lands in the same pipeline as every other lead.

  8. 08

    Review requests

    Happy new members get a short, friendly ask for a Google review at the right moment. Steady, genuine reviews help your Google Business Profile and give the next person comparing gyms a reason to pick yours.

Gym lead tracking Every lead, one board.

A pipeline is only useful if it matches how people actually join your gym. Ours starts from seven stages and gets adjusted to your sales process. Each stage has a job, an owner and a workflow that fires when a lead enters it, so the board you open on Monday morning is the truth rather than a guess.

  1. New leadArrives from any source, stamped with where it came from. The first text fires.
  2. ContactedThe sequence is running. Replies stop it and flag a person.
  3. BookedAn intro is on the calendar. Confirmation and reminders are queued.
  4. ShowedThey came in. Your team runs the intro and marks the outcome.
  5. JoinedA new member. From month three we check whether they're still paying.
  6. No-showDidn't make it. A reschedule offer goes out automatically.
  7. LostNot now. Parked with a reason, ready for a later reactivation.

What's the difference between automation and lead follow-up?

Lead follow-up is the conversation: how fast a new enquiry hears from your gym and what they're told. Automation is the system underneath it: the workflows, pipeline stages and reminders that move every enquiry from new to booked to showed up, plus jobs like missed-call text-back and review requests, without anyone having to remember to do it.

What does the pipeline show me?

Every enquiry from your ads and website lands in one pipeline with its source, then moves through contacted, booked, showed up and joined. You can see exactly where people stall, and we report on members who sign and stay, not on lead counts.

Who builds and maintains the workflows?

We do. Everything is built and tested on a real phone before it goes live, then tuned as it runs: wording adjusted where conversations stall, timing changed where reminders are ignored. Your team handles the replies that need a person.

Does automation cost extra?

It's part of what we run, not a bolt-on, and it's set up in your free first month. Our fee in the free month is $0; you cover your own ad spend, paid straight to the platform and never marked up, plus minimal texting (SMS) costs.
