Local SEO for gyms isn't one ranking; it's showing up well in three places at once. Someone searching for a gym is usually close, ready to move and comparing a handful of options on their phone. The map results sit above the organic ones and cost nothing per click, which is why we start there and work outwards.

Found by more of the people nearby

  1. Google Business Profile, complete and alive

    The right primary and secondary categories, your services, real photos of your gym and coaches, accurate hours including holidays, and a description written for people, not keywords. A profile that looks open and cared for.

  2. Reviews, asked for the right way

    Reviews come from your members, so the habit has to live at your gym. We show your team when to ask, usually right after a member hits a milestone, how to make it a one-minute job with a direct link, and why every review deserves a reply, good or bad. We can also set up an automated review request, a short text with your direct review link, so asking doesn't depend on anyone remembering. Real reviews only: no buying, no swapping and no filtering who gets asked.

  3. Name, address and phone that match

    Your gym's name, address and phone number the same on your website, your profile and the main directories, so Google isn't left guessing which details are right.

  4. On-site foundations

    Titles and descriptions written for each page, clean heading structure, LocalBusiness schema, a sitemap and fast indexing. The groundwork Google reads before it decides what your pages are about.

  5. A fast site that ranks

    Most local searches happen on a phone, often on a weak connection. We hold pages to Google's Core Web Vitals, where under 2.5 seconds for the largest content to appear is the published "good" bar.

  6. Local pages, where they earn it

    If members travel in from neighbouring towns, a genuinely useful page for each one can help you show up there too. Only where it's justified: copy-paste city pages do more harm than good. The same goes for what you offer: a page that speaks plainly to people looking for a HIIT studio beats a list of every class under the sun.

SEO for fitness studios Honest about the clock.

SEO for fitness studios and gyms runs on two clocks. Fixes to your profile and site land within weeks; ranking movement takes months and depends on your competition, not on our enthusiasm. This is what the timeline realistically looks like, and it's part of how we work with every gym.

Month6The realistic window

  1. First weeks: audit and tidy

    We search for your gym the way a local would, check the profile, the reviews, your details across the web and how fast your site loads on a real phone. Then the profile gets fixed and the obvious site problems go first.

  2. Ongoing: freshness

  3. Month three to six: the realistic window

Gym SEO FAQ Straight answers.

What gym owners ask before they spend time on local search. For the offer itself, see the full FAQ.

Can you get my gym to number one on Google Maps?

We won't promise a position, because nobody controls that and anyone who promises one is guessing. We do the foundations properly: a complete profile, real reviews, consistent details and a fast, well-structured site. Those genuinely matter, and they keep paying off.

How long does gym SEO take?

Profile and site fixes land within weeks. Ranking movement is slower: month three to six is the realistic window, and only alongside a maintained profile and reviews. How fast depends mostly on how competitive your area is.

How do we get more Google reviews?

Ask consistently, at the right moment, and make it easy: a personal ask when a member is proudest of the place, with a direct link so it takes a minute. We can set up an automated review request so every new member gets that ask without anyone having to remember it. Then reply to every review, good or bad. Never buy reviews or only ask people you expect to be positive. Our Google Business Profile guide for gyms includes a review-ask text you can copy.

Do I need a new website for SEO?

Not always. Most local SEO problems are page titles, heading structure, speed and details that don't match your Google Business Profile, and fixing those on your current site is usually cheaper and faster than a rebuild. If your site sits on a platform we can't make fast, we'll say so.

Should I do SEO or Google Ads first?

Your Google Business Profile is worth fixing regardless, because the map results cost nothing per click. Ads bring people in this week while SEO builds. For most gyms the answer is both, with the profile tidied in the same week the ads go live.
