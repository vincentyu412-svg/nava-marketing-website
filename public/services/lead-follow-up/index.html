Gym lead follow-up, where the sale is won.

We build and run the system that stops it: text, email and voicemail that reach every enquiry within minutes, a sequence that keeps going for weeks, and a human handover when the conversation turns real.

Speed to lead Minutes, not hours.

Speed to lead is the oldest finding in sales and still the most ignored. Harvard Business Review's study of online sales leads found firms that tried to contact a lead within an hour were nearly seven times more likely to qualify it than those that waited even an hour longer, and more than sixty times more likely than those that waited a day. It isn't a fitness study and the exact ratios won't transfer, but the direction holds in every gym we've worked in.

You can fix that with discipline and a person who never takes a day off, or you can automate the first hour and keep the humans for the conversations that need one. That's what this service is, and it's why gym lead response time is the first number we look at on the call.

Speed to lead: the first five minutes

The moment a lead arrives from any source, whether that's a Facebook form, your website, a Google Ads for gyms call, a walk-in or a referral, a text goes out in your gym's name from a local number, written the way a person would write it. Email follows, and a voicemail lands if they don't reply. It runs at 11pm on a Sunday too. And when a call to your gym goes unanswered, missed-call text-back replies by text within moments, so the caller doesn't just dial the next gym.

The sequence, not the single text

A first message never gets all the replies. The rest come later. We build a sequence that runs for weeks, with a different message, a different angle and a different time of day each time, and it stops the moment someone replies or books. Most gyms give up after a couple of attempts. The surprising sign-ups come later.

A CRM set up so it can be worked

A pipeline with stages that match how your gym actually sells: new lead, contacted, booked, showed, joined, no-show, lost. Every lead is stamped with its source, and tasks land on someone's phone. If your gym software can do the job we work inside it, and bolt on only what it can't do.

Booking, reminders and no-show recovery

A calendar with your real availability that leads book themselves into. Then a confirmation, a reminder the night before, a reminder an hour out, and a reschedule offer the moment someone doesn't turn up. In most gyms we look at, no-shows leak more members than bad leads do. It matters even more in 24 hour gyms, where nobody is on the desk at night to catch the booking.

The database nobody is working

Old enquiries, expired trials, members who left on good terms. We import the list, clean it, and run a written reactivation campaign against it, carefully, so it doesn't read as spam. It's the only part of gym marketing that can produce members with no ad spend at all.

Scripts, training and a read on the threads

Automation starts the conversation; a person still has to close it. You get the text replies, the call opener, objection answers and the intro structure written down, plus a short session with whoever works the leads. Start with our gym front desk sales script.

Gym lead follow-up FAQ Gym lead follow-up, answered.

What owners ask before they let software text their leads. More answers on the gym marketing FAQ, or bring your own question to the call.

Will the follow-up texts sound like a robot?

Straight answer, right here

Will the follow-up texts sound like a robot?

They're written to read like a person who works at your gym, sent from a local number, and they stop the instant someone replies. We write them in your voice and you approve every message before it goes out. If a sequence starts feeling like spam, that's a wording problem and we fix it in the weekly read.

Do I have to change my gym software?

Not necessarily. If your current platform handles pipelines, automated texting and booking properly, we work inside it. If it can't, we run the follow-up layer alongside it and keep your billing and class booking where they are.

Is automated texting even legal?

Business texting in the US and Canada is legal with consent and proper registration. We register your number for business messaging, make sure your forms capture consent clearly, honor opt-outs automatically, and keep sending inside reasonable hours. We aren't lawyers, and we'll tell you to check with yours on anything unusual.

Who actually answers when a lead replies?

Your team. The automation handles the first touch, the reminders and the chasing; a person at your gym takes the conversation once it's real. We give them the scripts and the training, and we'll tell you honestly if the person you've assigned isn't working the leads.

What about the leads already sitting in a spreadsheet?

We import and clean them, then run a reactivation campaign. Results vary hugely with how old and how warm the list is, so we won't put a number on it, but they're the cheapest members you'll ever get, because you already paid for those leads once.

Can I buy gym lead follow-up without the ads?

Yes, and for some gyms it's the right first move. If you're already getting walk-ins, referrals and website enquiries and losing them to slow follow-up, fixing that before you buy traffic is the cheaper order to do things in.
