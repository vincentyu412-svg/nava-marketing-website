Gym marketing budget calculator Start with the numbers.

How much should your gym spend on marketing? Put in four numbers (members, average fee, growth target and churn) and the calculator gives you a monthly budget, the most you can afford to pay for a new member, and how long that member takes to pay you back. Every line of the arithmetic is printed underneath, so you can disagree with any of it.

Average fee$120
New members / mo10

Payback0.9 mo

budget = min(10% of revenue, signups × ceiling)
