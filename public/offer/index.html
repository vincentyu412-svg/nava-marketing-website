Free trial questions The fine print.

There isn't much of it, but here it is in plain words. For everything else, from services to results, see the full gym marketing FAQ.

Do I need a credit card to claim the free month?

Not to book. The qualification call is free, 30 minutes on Google Meet with Vincent, and no card is needed. During the free month you pay GymPropel no management fee at all. What you do pay is your own ad spend, straight to the platform from your own ad account, plus minimal texting (SMS) costs for the lead follow-up.

How do I cancel?

Tell Vincent by text, email (vincent@gympropel.com) or phone, whichever is easiest. There's no cancellation fee and no contract to buy your way out of. If you don't cancel before your 30 days are up, you simply carry on month to month, with a monthly management fee you'll have the figure for from the call, and you can still stop whenever you like. Your ad account, pixel and audiences stay with you either way.

Why do you only take one gym per market?

Because we never want to work for your competitor. If two gyms in the same town hired us, our own ads would bid against each other for the same people, and our advice would pull in two directions. So if a gym in your market already works with us, we'll say so on the call. If you join, the gym down the road can't.
