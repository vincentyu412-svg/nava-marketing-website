None of them is clever on its own. The value is that all three exist at once, built in one pass and switched on together, which is rare. Ads bring strangers who live close enough to train, the page turns a tap into a booked intro, and follow-up answers within minutes while the owner is still coaching. Gym marketing results FAQ Results, answered.

The questions gym owners ask after watching the videos. If yours isn't here, bring it to the call.

Are these results typical?

They're real, and they're counted from launch, but they aren't a promise. Once it's running, gyms typically see 5–15 new members a month, and the first new member usually comes in around 5 days. Typical ranges, not guarantees; we'll look at your market on the call. The Puravida Fitness figure is our own estimate.

What did you actually run for these gyms?

The same three pieces at every gym, switched on together: Meta ads aimed at a real driving radius, a fast page built to book an intro, and automated follow-up by text, email and voicemail that replies within minutes.

How are these results counted?

From the day each campaign went live, and every number comes with its timeframe. We count members and money in the door, not leads: booked intros, shows and sign-ups, and from month three, how many of the people we brought in are still paying.

What kinds of gyms are these?

Two CrossFit affiliates (CrossFit Prior Lake and CrossFit Shelby), a HIIT studio (HIITCore Fitness), a strength and conditioning gym (ION Strength & Conditioning), a general fitness gym (MUV The Gym) and Puravida Fitness, in different markets. One gym per market, always.
