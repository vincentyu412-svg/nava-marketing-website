HIIT studio business How a HIIT studio sells.

HIIT is an easy sell and a hard keep. The workout does most of the persuading, so the real job is getting people through the door for a first class and back for a second. Here is how the business of a HIIT studio usually works, and what each part means for the way we market it.

Class playlist128 bpmRound / 04

Capacity is fixed, class by class

Every class has a set number of bikes, rowers or floor spots, and the timetable repeats every week. An empty spot at 6pm can't be sold again later. That makes class fill the number that matters, far more than a vague count of leads in a spreadsheet.

We find the classes that run thin and point ads and follow-up at those times first.

The intro offer is the front door

Most HIIT studios open with a free class, a trial week or a short challenge. The offer decides who turns up: people shopping for a bargain, or people ready to commit to training. A cheap offer that attracts the wrong crowd fills the room once and empties it by the end of the month.

We help you pick an intro that attracts people who plan to stay, then build every ad around it.

Energy sells, when people can see it

The music, the coach calling the rounds, a room full of people working hard: HIIT is visual, and short vertical video shows it better than any headline. Ads that feel like the class tend to beat stock photos and discount banners, and they pre-sell the experience before anyone walks in.

We give you a simple shot list to film on a phone and turn it into ad creative.

The drop-off happens after class one

Plenty of people love their first HIIT class and never book a second. Soreness, a busy week or no clear next step is usually enough. Studios that book the next class before people leave, and check in the day after, keep far more of the people they paid to attract.

Our follow-up and sign-up process covers the gap between first class and membership.

What intro offer works best for a HIIT studio?

It depends on your price point and how full your classes already are. A free first class keeps the barrier low, while a paid trial week or a short challenge tends to attract people who are more committed. We look at what you run today on the call and suggest the offer that fits your studio, not simply the cheapest one.

Can you fill our off-peak HIIT classes first?

Yes. The follow-up messages offer the class times you want filled first, and ad copy can feature them. If your mornings are full and midday classes run thin, the booking conversation steers new people toward the midday slots.

How fast will a new lead hear from my studio?

Within minutes. Every enquiry gets an automated text, email and voicemail, and the follow-up keeps going for weeks for anyone who goes quiet. You or your team can step into any conversation whenever you like.
