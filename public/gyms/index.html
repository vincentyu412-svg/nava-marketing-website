Gym marketing by gym type Five ways to sell a membership.

Each gym type has its own buyer, its own first step and its own bottleneck. A class studio runs out of spots, a PT studio runs out of trainer hours, and a 24/7 gym mostly runs out of time to answer. Each page below explains how that model usually works and exactly how we set up ads, follow-up and the first visit for it.

01 · Class-based

HIIT studio marketing

HIIT studios sell a timetable of high-energy classes with a fixed number of spots. We point ads and follow-up at the classes that run thin, build creative from your real sessions and close the gap between a great first class and a second booking.

How we market HIIT studios 02 · Schedule and community

Group fitness marketing

Group fitness studios fill dozens of classes a week and keep members through instructors and community. We steer bookings toward quiet classes, feature your instructors and move intro-pack buyers toward membership.

How we market group fitness studios 03 · Coached, premium

CrossFit marketing

Affiliates sell coaching and community at a higher price, usually through a free intro and an on-ramp. Everything we run aims at a booked intro with a coach, with follow-up that answers while the owner is on the floor.

How we market CrossFit affiliates 04 · Access-controlled

24 hour gym marketing

24/7 gyms sell convenient access, compete with clubs a few minutes away and are often unstaffed for part of the day. We sell location and hours, answer enquiries at any hour and make tours and joining easy from a phone.

How we market 24/7 gyms 05 · High-ticket, one-to-one

Personal training marketing

PT studios sell trainer hours at a high price, and the sale happens in a consultation. We build ads and follow-up to book consults with trainers who have room, with reminders so those consults actually show up.

How we market PT studios Something in between?

Fitness studio marketing FAQ Straight answers.

Common questions about how we work with different gym types. The full gym marketing FAQ covers contracts, billing and the free month.

Do you only work with these five gym types?

No. These are the most common models, and many gyms mix them, like a CrossFit box with personal training or a studio with an open floor. If your gym has an existing member base and can fund its own ad spend, we will look at it on the call and tell you honestly whether we can help.

What does every gym type get?

The same core system: Meta ads within driving distance, a fast website, lead follow-up within minutes, reminders and an intro built to sell, and Vincent, the founder, as your strategist. Google ads are added where local search demand makes them worth it. What changes by gym type is the offer, the message and the follow-up.

Is the free month the same for every gym type?

Yes. Whatever your gym type, the first month is free: no contract, no deposit and no upfront cost. Our fee for the first 30 days is $0. You cover your own ad spend, paid straight to the platform and never marked up, plus minimal texting costs. After that it is month to month, and we still work with one gym per market, so the gym down the road can't hire us.
