09 FAQ

Straight answers.

Six things to settle before you claim your free month. More in the full gym marketing FAQ.

01/06
Do you require contracts, deposits, or upfront costs?

No. Nothing upfront. The only cost in your free month is your own ad spend, paid straight to Meta. We never touch it or mark it up.

How are you able to offer one month free?

Because we're confident in the system. It's the same proven process for every gym, and we'd rather prove it with real members than with a pitch. If it works, you stay. If not, you walk away owing nothing.

What makes you different from other gym marketing agencies?

Ads, follow-up and your website run together as one system. We report on members who sign and stay, not lead counts. You deal with the founder directly. And we work with one gym per market.

What are the qualifications?

You run a gym with an existing member base, you can fund your own ad spend, and nobody in your market already works with us. If you're a very small gym in a tiny town, paid ads might not be your bottleneck. We'll tell you on the call.

What happens after our first month?

We review the numbers together. Happy? Continue month to month and cancel whenever. Not happy? Walk away and owe nothing.

How long does it take to see results?

Campaigns go live within days. The first new member typically comes in around 5 days, and gyms typically see 5–15 new members a month once it's running.
