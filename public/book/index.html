Booking FAQ Before you book.

More answers on the gym marketing FAQ, or see exactly what's in the free first month.

Is there a fee if we start working together?

Not in month one: our fee is $0. You cover your own ad spend, paid straight to the platform and never marked up, plus minimal texting costs. After that it's month to month.

Am I locked in once I start?

No. There's no contract and no deposit. If you want to stop, cancel by text, email or phone, whichever is easiest.

Could a gym near me already be working with you?

Possibly. We take one gym per market, so we never work for your competitor. Vincent checks whether your market is still open on the call.

What should I have ready for the call?

Three rough numbers: how many paying members you have, what a member pays you each month, and last month's ad spend. Zero is a perfectly good answer. Want to run the numbers first? Try the gym marketing budget calculator.
