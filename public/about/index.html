They sent a lead count. Then they went quiet. We answer for members.

Vincent Yu founded GymPropel to fix the thing gym owners complain about most when they talk about agencies. The monthly report says the campaign is working. There are plenty of leads and plenty of clicks. But the floor doesn't change, the member count doesn't move, and nobody at the agency can say why.

Leads were never the problem on their own. An enquiry nobody answers for a day goes cold. An intro nobody reminds doesn't show up. An ad that sends people to a slow page on their phone loses them before they ever ask a question. When the ads, the follow-up and the website are run by different people, or not really run at all, every hand-off is another place to lose the sale.

So we run all three as one system and judge ourselves on the number a gym owner actually banks: members who sign and stay. It's why the first month is free and there's no contract. We would rather prove it with real members than with a pitch.
